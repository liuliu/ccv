NAInt8MatMul dynamic-M range normalization, 2026-10-05
Baseline: b2ffdba5bf00733e876397515d9b2918d87ee215
Branch: m5-na-int8-matmul

Minimal production change: normalize dynamic M to 0 below 4096 and to
4096 at/above 4096 in both descriptor equality and std::hash. Restore
the corresponding native groupM selection in the kernel descriptor.
Static M remains exact. The shared cache and shader generator are unchanged.
Dynamic-M register selection remains disabled as previously agreed.
The 36-core moreNumberOfTiles cutoff and other device gates are unchanged.

Validation:
- Max: make debug -j4 passed; descriptor-cache checks for 10/35/36/40/80
  core profiles and 18 direct register configurations passed. The latter
  include 36 plain/rotated CPU-reference GEMMs and 54 reused-row checks.
- Max: dynamic_m_bench 1 passed all 24 combinations (8 M values, 3
  layouts), with bit-exact static/dynamic outputs and CPU sample checks.
  It covers contiguous, batched, and interleaved layouts with bias.
- Max: both focused rowwise INT8 GEMM integration tests passed.
- iPad: the same cache/register checks and 24 dynamic-M combinations
  passed in a signed standalone MFA app. This is not a full iOS suite.
- Pipeline checks verify reuse within each M range, a distinct pipeline
  across 4096, and reuse when returning to the lower range.

Paired GPU timings include quantization and GEMM, all with loadM=true.
N=1536, K=6144. Baseline/update/update/baseline order. Max uses 15 samples
per launch, >=0.3 GPU-second warmup, 10-second idle gaps; iPad uses 21
samples, >=0.5 GPU-second warmup, 20-second idle gaps. All launches began
and ended at nominal thermal state. Full output hashes match per shape.

Small changes within repeat variation are not established speedups.
This change restores native row grouping; it does not address the larger
performance gaps from keeping dynamic M on native operands.
