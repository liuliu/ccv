AttentionR1 decode parity review, 2026-10-07

Machine: Apple M5 Ultra, 80 GPU cores, 256 GiB, macOS 27.0.1.
CCV branch: m5-attention-r1-parity, based on bd7f3d37.
MLX: 25532871329fe4cafdada9fa0941a1cdbfb3a9f2, actual SDPA primitive,
with the archived D256 JIT signature compatibility patch.

Scope: FP16 decode GPU time, R=1 and R=2, D=128/256, contexts through
65536. The permanent suite also validates BF16. H16/Hkv4/D256 and
H24/Hkv4/D256 match Local Code Qwen3.5 4B/9B and 27B configurations.
See model-reference.txt for the exact source revision and call site.

Final matched-input comparison: 72 shapes, 438 processes,
three implementations, two reversed passes, 64 samples per process,
two GPU seconds warmup. All recorded thermal states are nominal.
The final CSV uses the final policy timings for five shapes. For 67 shapes,
the actual C++ selector and unchanged shader source prove identical GPU
configurations, allowing the earlier measurements to carry forward.
pre-policy-comparison.csv preserves the earlier gap and all flags.
Medians are recomputed from raw samples with the same convention for
CCV and MLX. No measurement is silently discarded.

The full comparison.csv contains every measured shape and both current
process medians. audit.json lists all material flags (>5 percent AND
>2 microseconds), plus separate single-batch and multi-batch summaries.
Material MLX gaps: 1; development-baseline flags: 1.

The final previous_candidate column refers to the earlier group4
register-based candidate, not unchanged unstable. Historical initial /
validation data separately record the unchanged unstable comparison.
Read phase-key.txt before combining evidence across development phases.

MLX uses stride-only views of the same B/S/H/D input storage. For B>1,
its vector attention path creates internal contiguous Q/K/V copies;
those are inside the timing. Batch speedups describe the complete
operation, including those copies. CCV adds no physical transpose.

Implementation and selection rationale are in implementation.txt and
gating-review.txt: register-resident query/state, related GQA head reuse,
workload/core-count split selection, a parallel FP32 merge, and a
single-dispatch cooperative kernel for short contexts. The last refinement
shortens single-head traversal when B*R*Hq is below GPU core count; it
retains the dense-query policy after the forced B4 split regressed.
No model-name, M5, or Ultra exception is used. Constants were calibrated
on this host; another physical M5 must validate performance there.

Correctness: 116 permanent shapes in each of FP16/BF16 (232 unique
combinations, 234 processes including a rerun). Every process has a full
finiteness scan and sampled double-precision CPU reference.
Integration tests compare full randomized outputs with the CPU backend,
including dynamic cache transitions, offset/in-place
guards, empty causal rows, odd GQA, large finite V, sinks, and saved LSE.
See integration/expanded-tests.json and final-tests.json in the archive.

Build and suite status: targeted debug library and MPS binary builds
passed; the full DNN suite passed 126/126. The generic debug make target
encountered an unrelated missing wget during VGG sample download.
The full BLAS attempt stopped at the H256 rowwise-x GEMM fallback test
with a Metal simdgroup_matrix_storage address-space compilation error.
The same failure was reproduced on clean bd7f3d37. This is not a claim
that the full BLAS suite passed. Focused decode filters passed; exact
commands, outputs and source/binary hashes are preserved.

Remaining repeatable MLX gap:
R2/C32768/B1/H16:8/D128: CCV 139.333 us versus MLX 130.730 us,
6.58 percent slower. The two CCV process medians were 139.542 and
139.125 us. This is separate from the intermittent observations below.
Near-parity across the suite does not mean every shape reaches parity.

Intermittent timing observations:
R1/C32768/H64:4/D128 in paired-focus once measured 348 us. Six fresh
process rechecks (including the exact original binary) gave 149-151 us,
and the final comparison repeated the fast result. The original sample
and flag remain in the archive; its cause was not established.
R2/C2049/B2/H24:4/D256 noncausal in final measured 80.875 us on pass 0
and 36.375 us on pass 1. The two-pass aggregate retains that regression
flag against the earlier candidate. Separate six-process rechecks for
each implementation are preserved in outlier-recheck.
  fp16: process medians (us): 36.625, 36.396, 36.625, 36.875, 36.500, 36.500
  previous_fp16: process medians (us): 36.500, 36.333, 36.625, 36.500, 36.667, 36.667
  mlx: process medians (us): 81.375, 81.062, 81.396, 81.500, 81.229, 80.854
These observations do not establish a specific hardware or allocator
