Saved evidence and comparison controls

final:
  fp16: candidate before the final sparse-query traversal guard, base bd7f3d37.
  previous_fp16: earlier register-based candidate with four logical heads per
    workgroup but one head per SIMD (group4-source snapshot). This is a strong
    development baseline, not unchanged unstable.
  mlx: actual MLX 25532871329fe4cafdada9fa0941a1cdbfb3a9f2 plus the archived
    D256 JIT signature patch. No selector overrides or manual input conversion.
    MLX internal layout copies, when selected for batches >1, are timed.
  Two passes, reversed case/variant order, 64 samples, two GPU seconds warmup.

gqa2-final / gqa2-numerical:
  Six Hq/Hkv=16/8, D128 shapes exercise the one-SIMD workgroup boundary.
  Both precisions passed; two timed passes with group4 and actual MLX completed.

numerical-final:
  96 permanent shapes in each of FP16 and BF16, one pass, eight timing samples,
  0.01 GPU seconds warmup. These 192 runs are correctness coverage only; their
  cold timings are not evidence of parity or regression.

paired-focus:
  20 D128 shapes, current paired-head candidate versus group4 and actual MLX.
  One pass, 64 samples, two GPU seconds warmup. One R1/C32768/H64:4 result was
  348 us and flagged a regression. The raw flag remains in the saved evidence.

private-repeats:
  Three fresh processes with the diagnostic application (2,2,5 GPU seconds
  warmup), and three with the exact original paired-focus binary (2 seconds),
  rechecked R1/C32768/H64:4. All six gave approximately 149-151 us. A cause for
  the original slow process was not established. Do not silently discard it.

profile-private:
  Stage profiling now uses context::request_scratch, matching production's
  private, capacity-rounded allocation. Production and selected complete
  dispatches agree on the checked long contexts. Isolated stage times are
  diagnostic and should not be added or compared to complete MLX SDPA.

profile-ablation:
  Same-source one-head/two-head controls with matching partitions. These
  precede the scratch-allocation correction; selected stages used a separate
  shared scratch buffer. They establish hypotheses, with production results
  and profile-private supplying the later validation.

integration:
  Final candidate: full DNN suite, five focused decode filters, and full BLAS
  attempt. Baseline GEMM shader failure reproduces on clean bd7f3d37. The old
  generic attention-sinks shader failure also has an unchanged-baseline log.
  Expanded cache and FP16/BF16 saved-logsumexp tests are rebuilt after the
  final selector change; expanded-tests.json records those results and the
  final DNN rerun. Inspect their exit codes before claiming a passing result.

Source/binary hashes and exact commands accompany each phase. The final CSV
uses the usual median of all 64 raw samples, then the median across two process
medians. The archived MLX executable itself prints the upper middle sample;
recomputing from raw samples gives both implementations the same convention.
A material comparison flag requires >5 percent and >2 us additional GPU time.

initial / validation:
  Earlier development phases, retained to document the change from unchanged
  unstable bd7f3d37. Their previous_fp16 binary is the clean unstable build,
  unlike final's group4 development baseline. Its binary hash and clean-source
  provenance are verified in unstable-baseline-metadata.json. Initial used
  32 samples and 0.25 GPU seconds warmup; validation used 64 and one second.
  These runs contain earlier candidate code and are historical evidence, not
  the final parity measurements. Do not substitute their candidate medians
  for the final two-pass comparison.

profile-long-d256 / profile-cap-controls:
  Same shader, FP32 state, two GPU seconds warmup and 64 samples per stage.
  These expose the tradeoff between shorter serial traversal and merge work.
  A uniform 128-row limit was rejected after the B4 control regressed about
  26 percent. The final policy shortens single-head traversal only when
  independent queries are fewer than GPU cores. All raw controls are retained.

policy-final / policy-numerical:
  Five shapes compared against the same group4 and MLX controls: the affected
  original 64K case and four batch/MHA controls. The numerical supplement has
  15 shapes in both precisions: ten timed shapes previously missing from the
  permanent numerical manifest, four new controls, and the affected old case.
  The final permanent manifest has 116 shapes, 232 precision combinations.
  There are 234 numerical processes including the rechecked original case.

Final comparison.csv:
  The evidence_phase column identifies which phase measured each row.
  policy-final supplies five rows; 67 rows reuse earlier measurements only
  after comparing the actual before/after C++ selector results and proving
  identical GPU source and pipeline geometry. selection-audit.json records
  all 232 selections. pre-policy-comparison.csv preserves the original
  5.36-percent MLX gap and the intermittent baseline regression flag.
