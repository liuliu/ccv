tiled_linear: previous coalesced single-pass candidate, 16.5 KiB static threadgroup memory.

small_128/small_256: coalesced single-pass Morton variants reducing in SIMD groups first; 512 B/1 KiB scratch.
  These change summation order and are checked for finite output and relative L2 <1e-4 against production means.
  Other variants must match production means byte-for-byte. This is a mean-only check, not full attention validation of the changed rounding.
All median timings and paired speedups exclude warmups. Speedup is median within-round production-time/candidate-time.

Outcome: production remains unchanged. Actual native is about 3.5-4.4x slower by median timing on the long shapes.
Morton ordering modestly improves tiled locality but does not beat the two-pass reduction.
The best smaller-scratch variant is still around 5-7% slower; long-shape mean relative L2 is at most 1.42e-7 for small_256.
There is no end-to-end speedup claim and no production use of the candidates.
