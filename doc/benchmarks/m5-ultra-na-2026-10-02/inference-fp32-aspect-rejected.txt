These results reject a blanket accumulator-mode exemption; they do not justify
a special case for one Local Code matrix. The native MPP path is faster on
several larger shapes even with explicit FP32 accumulation, sometimes faster
than actual MLX. The follow-up tests native tensor base-pointer reuse instead
of adding a narrowly selected register exception. That is a separate experiment.

Three warm iterations, >=.5 GPU seconds warmup, seven samples, two reversed
orders. GPU work is serial and follows all CPU compilation. Weights are already
expanded; bias/decode/casts are excluded. Explicit low-precision controls keep
their original route. The current production library and source remain unchanged.
Complete sources, commands, per-order results, logs and binary hashes are saved.
